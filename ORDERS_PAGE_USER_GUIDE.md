# Orders Page - Quick Start Guide

## 🎯 Feature: Place Orders from Your Cart

Products you add to your cart are automatically reflected on the Orders page, allowing you to complete your purchase with just a few clicks.

---

## 📋 Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Login to Your Account
```
URL: http://localhost:5175/login

Email: admin@example.com
Password: admin123
```

### Step 2: Add Products to Cart
- Browse the homepage or product pages
- Click "Add to Cart" on any product
- Products will be added to your shopping cart
- You'll see a cart count in the navbar

### Step 3: Navigate to Orders Page
```
URL: http://localhost:5175/orders
```

### Step 4: Choose Your Tab

#### Tab 1: "My Orders" 📦
- View all your previous orders
- See order status and tracking information
- Click to view order details
- Track delivery progress

#### Tab 2: "Place Order" (Cart)
- Shows all products you added to cart
- Each item displays:
  - Product image
  - Product name
  - Price
  - Quantity controls (-, qty, +)
  - Remove button
  - Total for that item

### Step 5: Review Order Summary
The sidebar shows:
```
Items (2):              ₹23,000
Shipping:              FREE
Tax (18%):            ₹4,140.00
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Total:                ₹27,140.00
```

### Step 6: Fill in Delivery Details

**Shipping Address** (Required)
```
Example:
123 Main Street
City, State 12345
Country
```

**Payment Method** (Required)
- 💳 Credit/Debit Card
- 📱 UPI
- 🏦 Net Banking
- 💵 Cash on Delivery

**Coupon Code** (Optional)
- Enter if you have a valid coupon

### Step 7: Place Order
- Click **"Place Order & Proceed to Checkout"** button
- Order will be created
- You'll be redirected to order confirmation page
- You'll see your Order Number and details

### Step 8: Confirm Order
- View order confirmation
- Check order number and amount
- See estimated delivery date
- Download invoice (if available)

## 💡 Tips & Tricks

### Continue Shopping
- While on Orders page, click "Continue Shopping" to go back to homepage
- Add more products and they'll appear in your cart automatically

### Modify Quantity
- Use +/- buttons on each item to adjust quantity
- Changes take effect immediately

### Remove Item
- Click "Remove" button on any item to remove from cart
- Item is removed instantly

### Cart Persistence
- Your cart is saved even if you close browser
- Cart items remain available when you login next time

### Track Orders
- Go to Orders page
- Click on "My Orders" tab
- Click on any order to see tracking details
- See real-time delivery status

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Can I save items for later?**
A: Yes! Items in your cart are saved automatically.

**Q: Can I change my address after placing order?**
A: Contact customer support for address changes.

**Q: Can I cancel my order?**
A: Orders can be canceled within 1 hour of placement. Check "My Orders" for cancel option.

**Q: Is there a minimum order value?**
A: No, you can order any amount.

**Q: Do I get free shipping?**
A: Yes! All orders have FREE shipping.

**Q: What payment methods are available?**
A: Credit/Debit Card, UPI, Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery.
